Moral and strategic dilemmas, measured

See how people really decide

Raylrode builds short, playable apps that put people in moral and strategic dilemmas. Each one records what people choose and how long they take to decide. The results feed social projects, classrooms and intelligence systems.

01

Social projects

Our first app

Swerve

Live

The trolley problem, one swipe at a time. Players face 20 dilemmas, swipe to act or to leave things as they are, and get a moral archetype at the end. They can then challenge friends to see where they agree.

  • Design18 balanced scenarios: lever or push, 1 to 5 lives at stake, and a stranger, child or family member on the other track
  • MeasuredEvery choice, with its decision time to the millisecond
  • QualityAttention checks, and a randomised swipe direction so habit can be told apart from intent
  • PrivacyProfile questions are optional, nicknames never leave the device, and IP addresses are never stored

The line

What we'll build next

Swerve is the first stop. These are the next ones: working titles, open to partners who want to shape them.

  1. Swerve

    LiveMoral

    Who would you save? Harm trade-offs in the trolley tradition.

  2. Commons

    PlannedStrategic

    A shared resource, a group of players, and a choice each round: contribute or take. Shows when groups cooperate, and what makes them stop.

    For community and climate projects, civic planning

  3. Triage

    PlannedMoral

    Not enough beds, budget or aid for everyone. Players decide who gets what, and why.

    For public health teaching, policy consultation

  4. Standoff

    PlannedStrategic

    Two players, one deal. Trust, bargain or betray, and see how it plays out across cultures and age groups.

    For economics and game theory classes, negotiation training

  5. Fog

    PlannedStrategic

    Decisions with missing or misleading information. People and AI systems face the same dilemmas, so their choices can be compared directly.

    For AI evaluation, decision-support research
